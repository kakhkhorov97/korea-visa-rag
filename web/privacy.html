Privacy and coverage

Public beta by Sean Kakhkhorov. Updated October 26, 2026.

Ask general questions

Do not enter names, passport or registration numbers, contact details, financial or medical information, case documents, or other sensitive information. There is no document-upload feature. A basic pattern check blocks some common identifiers. It cannot detect all personal information.

이름, 여권번호, 외국인등록번호, 연락처, 금융·의료 정보, 개인 사건 서류 등 개인정보나 민감정보를 입력하지 마세요. 간단한 패턴 검사는 모든 개인정보를 식별하지 못합니다.

Where your question goes

Source-only keyword search runs on the application's Google Cloud backend without calling Gemini. If you enable AI, your question and selected public source passages are sent to Google's Gemini API for answer generation. Meaning and combined search also send your question for embedding.

The existing Gemini project uses unpaid quota. Google's terms describe how inputs and outputs may be used to improve its products and reviewed by people, with regional exceptions. Do not submit sensitive, confidential or personal information. Read Gemini's current terms.

AI beta access

The AI beta is intended for people aged 18 or older using it from South Korea. Enabling AI is a self-declaration of those conditions; this app does not verify age or location. Users outside these conditions can use source-only keyword search. Broader AI availability requires a provider-access review.

Storage and logs

The app does not intentionally save question or answer bodies to a database or application logs. Conversation cards stay in the open page and disappear when it is refreshed. Usage protection stores counters and recent request timestamps. Google Cloud processes technical request metadata, including request URLs and network addresses. Avoid placing private information in URLs or direct API queries. Provider handling and retention follow Google's terms; this notice does not promise zero retention.

The main page loads IBM Plex Sans KR through Google Fonts, which makes requests to Google. Newsreader is bundled with the app. No analytics, advertising cookies or user accounts are implemented.

What the sources cover

The beta contains 44 selected statutory passages covering 21 broad visa categories, general residence procedures, fees, reductions and references to application-document annexes. These are selected excerpts, not a complete immigration database. Full subtype eligibility, occupation criteria and document checklists remain incomplete. Each passage includes its official link; the retrieval date records when it was collected, not a guarantee that it is still current.

AI explanations can be inaccurate even when a quotation matches. Exact source-quote checks do not prove a translation or legal interpretation. This is general information, not legal advice. Confirm decisions with the Immigration Contact Center at 1345 or HiKorea.

Report a problem

You can report a reproducible technical problem through the project's public issues. Do not post personal immigration details or private documents there.
