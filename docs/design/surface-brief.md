# Visa assistant web interface

Mode: Operate. Approved reference: docs/design/modern-demo-v1.png.
Keep the existing backend, endpoints, keys, model, source corpus and cloud limits.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bilingual question workspace with evidence beside the answer. Source
inspection is a first-class task, not an unreadable footer.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-blue structural navigation, warm stone ground, white answer
surface, sage evidence area, blue actions, humanist body type and a modest serif
heading. Numbered citations connect the answer to its original passage.

STORY: Choose a search method, ask a visa question, read the answer and inspect
its official evidence. A recruiter can try the app without an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height left rail, top status bar, compact heading, central
conversation and right evidence panel. Composer spans the workspace bottom.
On phones, controls become a disclosure and sources follow the conversation.

FORM: Approved architectural workspace mockup. The user's approval fixes the
composition. seed: none (user-approved image).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
